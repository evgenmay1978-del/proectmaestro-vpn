# ADR-0008 — desired-state user reconciliation

Status: proposed. Target-only; no deployment, audit result, measurement, or operational completion is claimed.

## Topic-specific target
desired-state user reconciliation

## Problem
Choose a safe target for desired-state user reconciliation without coupling it to ordinary VPN or claiming unobserved behavior.

## Constraints
The decision remains isolated, reversible, secret-free, and blocked from live use until its own evidence and same-turn approval gate exist.

## Alternatives
imperative mutation versus queued scripts versus desired-state reconciliation.

## Trade-offs
convergence is slower than direct mutation; this cost is accepted only if the isolation boundary is demonstrated.

## Risks
conflicting generation can apply stale intent; the risk remains an open blocker rather than an assumed success.

## Compatibility
ordinary users remain outside CDN desired state.

## Testing
Required evidence: partial node failure followed by deterministic convergence. The result is UNVERIFIED until a dated, redacted run is recorded.

## Rollback
previous desired generation for the entitlement.

## Decision
Propose desired-state reconciliation with generation checks after the required evidence and approval gates; do not treat this ADR as authorization.

## Evidence
The master requirements and local design constraints are inputs only. Evidence addressing conflicting generation can apply stale intent remains UNVERIFIED; live state, client behavior, timing, and rollback duration are unknown.
