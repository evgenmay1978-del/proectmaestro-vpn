# ADR-0014 — entitlement-only suspension

Status: proposed. Target-only; no deployment, audit result, measurement, or operational completion is claimed.

## Topic-specific target
entitlement-only suspension

## Problem
Choose a safe target for entitlement-only suspension without coupling it to ordinary VPN or claiming unobserved behavior.

## Constraints
The decision remains isolated, reversible, secret-free, and blocked from live use until its own evidence and same-turn approval gate exist.

## Alternatives
disable account versus hide all nodes versus entitlement-only suspension.

## Trade-offs
grace policy delays enforcement deliberately; this cost is accepted only if the isolation boundary is demonstrated.

## Risks
existing sessions may outlive publication removal; the risk remains an open blocker rather than an assumed success.

## Compatibility
ordinary VPN remains available.

## Testing
Required evidence: low-balance transition and replenishment resume. The result is UNVERIFIED until a dated, redacted run is recorded.

## Rollback
resume the entitlement without recreating identity.

## Decision
Propose entitlement-only suspension with bounded grace after the required evidence and approval gates; do not treat this ADR as authorization.

## Evidence
The master requirements and local design constraints are inputs only. Evidence addressing existing sessions may outlive publication removal remains UNVERIFIED; live state, client behavior, timing, and rollback duration are unknown.
