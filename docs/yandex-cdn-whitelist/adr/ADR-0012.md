# ADR-0012 — epoch-scoped cumulative delta

Status: proposed. Target-only; no deployment, audit result, measurement, or operational completion is claimed.

## Topic-specific target
epoch-scoped cumulative delta

## Problem
Choose a safe target for epoch-scoped cumulative delta without coupling it to ordinary VPN or claiming unobserved behavior.

## Constraints
The decision remains isolated, reversible, secret-free, and blocked from live use until its own evidence and same-turn approval gate exist.

## Alternatives
monotonic lifetime versus reset-to-zero versus epoch-scoped samples.

## Trade-offs
epoch storage and deduplication add state; this cost is accepted only if the isolation boundary is demonstrated.

## Risks
counter reset can look like negative usage; the risk remains an open blocker rather than an assumed success.

## Compatibility
historical usage remains immutable across restarts.

## Testing
Required evidence: duplicate sample replay and restart fixtures. The result is UNVERIFIED until a dated, redacted run is recorded.

## Rollback
open a new epoch without rewriting intervals.

## Decision
Propose epoch-scoped delta from cumulative samples after the required evidence and approval gates; do not treat this ADR as authorization.

## Evidence
The master requirements and local design constraints are inputs only. Evidence addressing counter reset can look like negative usage remains UNVERIFIED; live state, client behavior, timing, and rollback duration are unknown.
