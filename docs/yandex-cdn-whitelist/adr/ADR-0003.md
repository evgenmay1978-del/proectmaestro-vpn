# ADR-0003 — pinned Xray provenance

Status: proposed. Target-only; no deployment, audit result, measurement, or operational completion is claimed.

## Topic-specific target
pinned Xray provenance

## Problem
Choose a safe target for pinned Xray provenance without coupling it to ordinary VPN or claiming unobserved behavior.

## Constraints
The decision remains isolated, reversible, secret-free, and blocked from live use until its own evidence and same-turn approval gate exist.

## Alternatives
floating binary versus local build versus pinned checksum.

## Trade-offs
upgrade cadence becomes deliberate; this cost is accepted only if the isolation boundary is demonstrated.

## Risks
provenance gap or unreproducible binary; the risk remains an open blocker rather than an assumed success.

## Compatibility
config syntax must match the pinned version.

## Testing
Required evidence: reproduce the binary and compare checksum. The result is UNVERIFIED until a dated, redacted run is recorded.

## Rollback
prior pinned binary with its matching config.

## Decision
Propose pinned checksum tied to source and build recipe after the required evidence and approval gates; do not treat this ADR as authorization.

## Evidence
The master requirements and local design constraints are inputs only. Evidence addressing provenance gap or unreproducible binary remains UNVERIFIED; live state, client behavior, timing, and rollback duration are unknown.
