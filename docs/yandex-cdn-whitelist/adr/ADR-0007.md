# ADR-0007 — opaque stats identity

Status: proposed. Target-only; no deployment, audit result, measurement, or operational completion is claimed.

## Topic-specific target
opaque stats identity

## Problem
Choose a safe target for opaque stats identity without coupling it to ordinary VPN or claiming unobserved behavior.

## Constraints
The decision remains isolated, reversible, secret-free, and blocked from live use until its own evidence and same-turn approval gate exist.

## Alternatives
customer UUID versus email-derived ID versus opaque stats identity.

## Trade-offs
mapping table adds controlled state; this cost is accepted only if the isolation boundary is demonstrated.

## Risks
identity collision or accidental reuse; the risk remains an open blocker rather than an assumed success.

## Compatibility
subscription identity and billing identity stay separate.

## Testing
Required evidence: rotation fixture proving counters do not merge. The result is UNVERIFIED until a dated, redacted run is recorded.

## Rollback
revoke opaque user while preserving usage history.

## Decision
Propose opaque stats identity mapped only server-side after the required evidence and approval gates; do not treat this ADR as authorization.

## Evidence
The master requirements and local design constraints are inputs only. Evidence addressing identity collision or accidental reuse remains UNVERIFIED; live state, client behavior, timing, and rollback duration are unknown.
