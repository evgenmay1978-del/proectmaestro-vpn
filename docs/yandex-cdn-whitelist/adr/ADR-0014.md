# ADR-0014 — billing suspension

Status: proposed. No deployment or measured success is claimed.

## Problem
Decide billing suspension without exposing ordinary VPN to an unproven CDN transport.

## Constraints
Existing identities, ports, subscriptions, balances and ordinary access remain unchanged. Secrets and sensitive origin context stay out of derivative artifacts. Four independent targets are mandatory.

## Alternatives
Retain current production coupling; use a reversible isolated candidate; or take a manual/unpinned shortcut.

## Trade-offs
Isolation adds release/reconciliation work but limits blast radius and provides evidence; shortcuts reduce setup effort while increasing unbounded regression risk.

## Risks
Inventory may be stale; client/core semantics may differ; counters and routes may drift. Unknowns block a production decision.

## Compatibility
CDN nodes are additive only for ACTIVE entitlement and explicit preset; ordinary nodes remain unchanged.

## Testing
Require source-linked isolated test, relevant subscription/transport/metering check, and redacted release evidence before canary.

## Rollback
Keep a prior immutable release and disable only affected entitlement/edge; preserve ordinary access and financial history.

## Decision
Propose **suspend only the affected entitlement for balance/limit outcomes**.

## Evidence
Owner requirements and ADR map supply constraints; audit, compatibility and live results are UNVERIFIED until separately recorded.
