# ADR-0002 — 3x-ui raw-field preservation

Status: proposed. Target-only; no deployment, audit result, measurement, or operational completion is claimed.

## Topic-specific target
3x-ui raw-field preservation

## Problem
Choose a safe target for 3x-ui raw-field preservation without coupling it to ordinary VPN or claiming unobserved behavior.

## Constraints
The decision remains isolated, reversible, secret-free, and blocked from live use until its own evidence and same-turn approval gate exist.

## Alternatives
panel upgrade versus bypass versus round-trip raw fields.

## Trade-offs
schema preservation adds adapter maintenance; this cost is accepted only if the isolation boundary is demonstrated.

## Risks
field loss during parse and re-emit; the risk remains an open blocker rather than an assumed success.

## Compatibility
ordinary subscription output remains byte-equivalent.

## Testing
Required evidence: export/import fixture covering unknown fields. The result is UNVERIFIED until a dated, redacted run is recorded.

## Rollback
restore exported config and remove the adapter.

## Decision
Propose round-trip raw fields before any panel ownership after the required evidence and approval gates; do not treat this ADR as authorization.

## Evidence
The master requirements and local design constraints are inputs only. Evidence addressing field loss during parse and re-emit remains UNVERIFIED; live state, client behavior, timing, and rollback duration are unknown.
