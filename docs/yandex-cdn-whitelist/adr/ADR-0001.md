# ADR-0001 — sidecar/reconciler boundary

Status: proposed. Target-only; no deployment, audit result, measurement, or operational completion is claimed.

## Topic-specific target
sidecar/reconciler boundary

## Problem
Choose a safe target for sidecar/reconciler boundary without coupling it to ordinary VPN or claiming unobserved behavior.

## Constraints
The decision remains isolated, reversible, secret-free, and blocked from live use until its own evidence and same-turn approval gate exist.

## Alternatives
shared inbound versus one isolated sidecar per node versus deferral.

## Trade-offs
process isolation and release coordination; this cost is accepted only if the isolation boundary is demonstrated.

## Risks
configuration drift between four nodes; the risk remains an open blocker rather than an assumed success.

## Compatibility
ordinary VPN stays on the existing inbound.

## Testing
Required evidence: independent reconciliation under partial node failure. The result is UNVERIFIED until a dated, redacted run is recorded.

## Rollback
stop the sidecar and restore its last selected release.

## Decision
Propose isolated sidecar with reconciler ownership after the required evidence and approval gates; do not treat this ADR as authorization.

## Evidence
The master requirements and local design constraints are inputs only. Evidence addressing configuration drift between four nodes remains UNVERIFIED; live state, client behavior, timing, and rollback duration are unknown.
