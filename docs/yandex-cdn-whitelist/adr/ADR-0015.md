# ADR-0015 — official-prefix origin policy

Status: proposed. Target-only; no deployment, audit result, measurement, or operational completion is claimed.

## Topic-specific target
official-prefix origin policy

## Problem
Choose a safe target for official-prefix origin policy without coupling it to ordinary VPN or claiming unobserved behavior.

## Constraints
The decision remains isolated, reversible, secret-free, and blocked from live use until its own evidence and same-turn approval gate exist.

## Alternatives
fixed address list versus open origin versus official prefix source.

## Trade-offs
atomic allowlist needs a safe updater; this cost is accepted only if the isolation boundary is demonstrated.

## Risks
operator lockout from incomplete prefixes; the risk remains an open blocker rather than an assumed success.

## Compatibility
origin path changes must not touch ordinary ports.

## Testing
Required evidence: firewall dry-run and isolated restore proof. The result is UNVERIFIED until a dated, redacted run is recorded.

## Rollback
restore prior ruleset while retaining SSH.

## Decision
Propose official prefix source applied atomically after the required evidence and approval gates; do not treat this ADR as authorization.

## Evidence
The master requirements and local design constraints are inputs only. Evidence addressing operator lockout from incomplete prefixes remains UNVERIFIED; live state, client behavior, timing, and rollback duration are unknown.
