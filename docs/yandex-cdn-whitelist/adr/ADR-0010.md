# ADR-0010 — additive subscription rendering

Status: proposed. Target-only; no deployment, audit result, measurement, or operational completion is claimed.

## Topic-specific target
additive subscription rendering

## Problem
Choose a safe target for additive subscription rendering without coupling it to ordinary VPN or claiming unobserved behavior.

## Constraints
The decision remains isolated, reversible, secret-free, and blocked from live use until its own evidence and same-turn approval gate exist.

## Alternatives
replace subscription versus separate feed versus additive rendering.

## Trade-offs
duplicate control and escaping add renderer complexity; this cost is accepted only if the isolation boundary is demonstrated.

## Risks
escaping can corrupt third-party imports; the risk remains an open blocker rather than an assumed success.

## Compatibility
ordinary nodes preserve order and bytes.

## Testing
Required evidence: golden subscription across supported clients. The result is UNVERIFIED until a dated, redacted run is recorded.

## Rollback
remove only CDN nodes for the entitlement.

## Decision
Propose additive rendering after ordinary nodes after the required evidence and approval gates; do not treat this ADR as authorization.

## Evidence
The master requirements and local design constraints are inputs only. Evidence addressing escaping can corrupt third-party imports remains UNVERIFIED; live state, client behavior, timing, and rollback duration are unknown.
