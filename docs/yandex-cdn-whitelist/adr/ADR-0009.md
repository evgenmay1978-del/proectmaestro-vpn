# ADR-0009 — edge approval evidence

Status: proposed. Target-only; no deployment, audit result, measurement, or operational completion is claimed.

## Topic-specific target
edge approval evidence

## Problem
Choose a safe target for edge approval evidence without coupling it to ordinary VPN or claiming unobserved behavior.

## Constraints
The decision remains isolated, reversible, secret-free, and blocked from live use until its own evidence and same-turn approval gate exist.

## Alternatives
first DNS answer versus manual allowlist versus approved edge evidence.

## Trade-offs
probe window delays publication; this cost is accepted only if the isolation boundary is demonstrated.

## Risks
edge churn can invalidate a candidate; the risk remains an open blocker rather than an assumed success.

## Compatibility
SNI and Host semantics stay bound to the profile.

## Testing
Required evidence: time-separated probes before approval. The result is UNVERIFIED until a dated, redacted run is recorded.

## Rollback
quarantine the edge and unpublish it.

## Decision
Propose approved edge evidence from repeated observations after the required evidence and approval gates; do not treat this ADR as authorization.

## Evidence
The master requirements and local design constraints are inputs only. Evidence addressing edge churn can invalidate a candidate remains UNVERIFIED; live state, client behavior, timing, and rollback duration are unknown.
