# ADR-0004 — capability preset versioning

Status: proposed. Target-only; no deployment, audit result, measurement, or operational completion is claimed.

## Topic-specific target
capability preset versioning

## Problem
Choose a safe target for capability preset versioning without coupling it to ordinary VPN or claiming unobserved behavior.

## Constraints
The decision remains isolated, reversible, secret-free, and blocked from live use until its own evidence and same-turn approval gate exist.

## Alternatives
single universal preset versus per-client flags versus catalog versions.

## Trade-offs
preset catalog requires lifecycle discipline; this cost is accepted only if the isolation boundary is demonstrated.

## Risks
capability mismatch across clients; the risk remains an open blocker rather than an assumed success.

## Compatibility
client family receives only supported fields.

## Testing
Required evidence: client matrix for every preset version. The result is UNVERIFIED until a dated, redacted run is recorded.

## Rollback
disable the preset without removing ordinary nodes.

## Decision
Propose versioned capability preset selected explicitly after the required evidence and approval gates; do not treat this ADR as authorization.

## Evidence
The master requirements and local design constraints are inputs only. Evidence addressing capability mismatch across clients remains UNVERIFIED; live state, client behavior, timing, and rollback duration are unknown.
