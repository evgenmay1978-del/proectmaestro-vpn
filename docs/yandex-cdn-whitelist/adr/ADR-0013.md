# ADR-0013 — immutable financial ledger

Status: proposed. Target-only; no deployment, audit result, measurement, or operational completion is claimed.

## Topic-specific target
immutable financial ledger

## Problem
Choose a safe target for immutable financial ledger without coupling it to ordinary VPN or claiming unobserved behavior.

## Constraints
The decision remains isolated, reversible, secret-free, and blocked from live use until its own evidence and same-turn approval gate exist.

## Alternatives
direct balance edit versus mutable invoice versus immutable ledger entry.

## Trade-offs
adjustment rows increase record count; this cost is accepted only if the isolation boundary is demonstrated.

## Risks
duplicate charge from replay or racing collectors; the risk remains an open blocker rather than an assumed success.

## Compatibility
existing wallet remains the single balance source.

## Testing
Required evidence: idempotency replay with exact money units. The result is UNVERIFIED until a dated, redacted run is recorded.

## Rollback
compensating reversal instead of deletion.

## Decision
Propose immutable ledger entry with idempotency key after the required evidence and approval gates; do not treat this ADR as authorization.

## Evidence
The master requirements and local design constraints are inputs only. Evidence addressing duplicate charge from replay or racing collectors remains UNVERIFIED; live state, client behavior, timing, and rollback duration are unknown.
