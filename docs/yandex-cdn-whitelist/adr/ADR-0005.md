# ADR-0005 — idle downlink recovery

Status: proposed. Target-only; no deployment, audit result, measurement, or operational completion is claimed.

## Topic-specific target
idle downlink recovery

## Problem
Choose a safe target for idle downlink recovery without coupling it to ordinary VPN or claiming unobserved behavior.

## Constraints
The decision remains isolated, reversible, secret-free, and blocked from live use until its own evidence and same-turn approval gate exist.

## Alternatives
blind retry versus heartbeat versus explicit idle recovery.

## Trade-offs
heartbeat costs traffic and battery; this cost is accepted only if the isolation boundary is demonstrated.

## Risks
idle cutoff can strand a nominal session; the risk remains an open blocker rather than an assumed success.

## Compatibility
battery and background limits remain client-specific.

## Testing
Required evidence: long-lived stream across the measured idle boundary. The result is UNVERIFIED until a dated, redacted run is recorded.

## Rollback
standard fallback when recovery evidence fails.

## Decision
Propose idle recovery proven per client and path after the required evidence and approval gates; do not treat this ADR as authorization.

## Evidence
The master requirements and local design constraints are inputs only. Evidence addressing idle cutoff can strand a nominal session remains UNVERIFIED; live state, client behavior, timing, and rollback duration are unknown.
