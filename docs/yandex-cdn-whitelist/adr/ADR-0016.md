# ADR-0016 — gated canary progression

Status: proposed. Target-only; no deployment, audit result, measurement, or operational completion is claimed.

## Topic-specific target
gated canary progression

## Problem
Choose a safe target for gated canary progression without coupling it to ordinary VPN or claiming unobserved behavior.

## Constraints
The decision remains isolated, reversible, secret-free, and blocked from live use until its own evidence and same-turn approval gate exist.

## Alternatives
big-bang rollout versus permanent test cohort versus gated canary.

## Trade-offs
observation window slows rollout; this cost is accepted only if the isolation boundary is demonstrated.

## Risks
abort threshold may expose weak telemetry; the risk remains an open blocker rather than an assumed success.

## Compatibility
ordinary cohort remains unchanged.

## Testing
Required evidence: stage metrics and negative-path abort evidence. The result is UNVERIFIED until a dated, redacted run is recorded.

## Rollback
unpublish the canary and select prior release.

## Decision
Propose gated canary with explicit promotion criteria after the required evidence and approval gates; do not treat this ADR as authorization.

## Evidence
The master requirements and local design constraints are inputs only. Evidence addressing abort threshold may expose weak telemetry remains UNVERIFIED; live state, client behavior, timing, and rollback duration are unknown.
