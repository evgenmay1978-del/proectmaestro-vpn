# ADR-0011 — server-side counter source

Status: proposed. Target-only; no deployment, audit result, measurement, or operational completion is claimed.

## Topic-specific target
server-side counter source

## Problem
Choose a safe target for server-side counter source without coupling it to ordinary VPN or claiming unobserved behavior.

## Constraints
The decision remains isolated, reversible, secret-free, and blocked from live use until its own evidence and same-turn approval gate exist.

## Alternatives
client counters versus aggregate interface totals versus server-side counters.

## Trade-offs
stats polling creates staleness handling work; this cost is accepted only if the isolation boundary is demonstrated.

## Risks
attribution gap during collector outage; the risk remains an open blocker rather than an assumed success.

## Compatibility
third-party clients need no billing plugin.

## Testing
Required evidence: counter comparison against instance totals. The result is UNVERIFIED until a dated, redacted run is recorded.

## Rollback
pause meter posting without estimating usage.

## Decision
Propose server-side counters keyed by opaque user after the required evidence and approval gates; do not treat this ADR as authorization.

## Evidence
The master requirements and local design constraints are inputs only. Evidence addressing attribution gap during collector outage remains UNVERIFIED; live state, client behavior, timing, and rollback duration are unknown.
